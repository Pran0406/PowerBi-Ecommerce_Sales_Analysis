# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview  
Developed an interactive data analysis dashboard to evaluate business performance across revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and regional trends.  
The solution transforms raw data into actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📈 Key Insights  

### 🌍 Regional Performance  
Top 3 states contribute approximately **60–70% of total revenue**, indicating strong geographic concentration and expansion opportunities.

### 👥 Customer Analysis  
Top 5 customers generate nearly **35–45% of total sales**, highlighting dependency and retention opportunities.

### 📅 Profit Trends  
Monthly profit shows fluctuations of up to **20–30%**, revealing seasonal patterns and inefficiencies.

### 🛒 Category Performance  
A single category contributes over **50% of total sales volume**, indicating demand concentration.

### 💳 Payment Behavior  
**65–75% of transactions** are completed via digital payments (UPI/Card), reflecting a shift toward cashless methods.

### 📦 Sub-Category Profitability  
A few sub-categories drive the majority of profits, while others operate at a loss.
